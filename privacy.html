Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

gLinux provides a local Alpine Linux command line on your Mac. The Mac App Store edition runs on your device. We do not operate an account service or collect analytics, diagnostics, command history, or files from the app.

Files created in gLinux are stored in the app's local container. Commands you run may connect to websites and package repositories. Those destinations may receive network information and any data you choose to send them. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. gLinux does not send your commands or files to us.

You can manage local app data through macOS. For privacy questions, open an issue. GitHub issues are public; do not post passwords, personal information, or private files.

隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 4 日

gLinux 在 Mac 本机提供 Alpine Linux 命令行。Mac App Store 版本在设备本地运行。我们不提供账户服务，也不从应用收集分析数据、诊断数据、命令历史或文件。

用户在 gLinux 中创建的文件保存在应用的本地容器内。用户运行的命令可以连接网站和软件包仓库；这些目的地可能收到网络信息以及用户选择发送的数据，其处理方式由各自的隐私政策决定。gLinux 不会把这些命令或文件发送给我们。

用户可通过 macOS 管理本地应用数据。如对隐私政策有疑问，请创建 Issue。GitHub Issue 是公开的，请勿发布密码、个人信息或私人文件。
