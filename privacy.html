Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

gLinux provides local Linux application sandboxes, terminal shells, Dashboard and MCP task tools on your Mac. The Mac App Store edition runs on your device. We do not operate an account service or collect analytics, diagnostics, command history, or files from the app.

Sandbox files are stored in the app's local container or a storage folder you select. Shared Mac folders remain at the locations you select. Commands you run may connect to websites and package repositories. Those destinations may receive network information and any data you choose to send them. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. gLinux does not send your commands or files to us.

Shared Mac folders require your selection in the system folder picker. New mounts default to read-only; read-write access allows guest commands to modify the selected folder. Local bookmarks retain that authorization.

MCP returns task output and requested file information to the client you connect. Your chosen client may process that information using its own services; its privacy policy and settings apply. Dashboard resource readings and sandbox metadata remain local.

Apple handles Pro subscription purchases, payment and transaction verification through StoreKit under Apple's privacy policies. We do not receive your payment card details. When Pro expires, local sandbox files and configuration remain.

You can manage local app data through macOS. For privacy questions, open an issue. GitHub issues are public; do not post passwords, personal information, or private files.

隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 9 日

gLinux 在 Mac 本机提供 Linux 应用沙箱、终端、Dashboard 和 MCP 任务工具。Mac App Store 版本在设备本地运行。我们不提供账户服务，也不从应用收集分析数据、诊断数据、命令历史或文件。

沙箱文件保存在应用的本地容器或用户选择的存储目录内；共享 Mac 文件夹保留在用户选择的位置。用户运行的命令可以连接网站和软件包仓库；这些目的地可能收到网络信息以及用户选择发送的数据，其处理方式由各自的隐私政策决定。gLinux 不会把这些命令或文件发送给我们。

共享 Mac 目录须由用户在系统目录选择器中选择。新挂载默认只读；选择读写后，guest 命令可修改所选目录。本地 bookmark 保留该授权。

MCP 向用户连接的客户端返回任务输出及请求的文件信息。所选客户端可能通过其自身服务处理这些信息，应以该客户端的隐私政策和设置为准。Dashboard 资源读数和沙箱元数据保存在本地。

Apple 通过 StoreKit 处理 Pro 订阅购买、支付和交易验证，适用 Apple 的隐私政策。我们不会收到用户的银行卡信息。Pro 到期后，本地沙箱文件和配置仍保留。

用户可通过 macOS 管理本地应用数据。如对隐私政策有疑问，请创建 Issue。GitHub Issue 是公开的，请勿发布密码、个人信息或私人文件。
